## 📊 Database Contents

### **11 Materials with Scientific References:**

#### **1. Graphene (Monolayer)** 🔬
- **Source:** Ferrari et al., Phys. Rev. Lett. 97, 187401 (2006)
- **DOI:** 10.1103/PhysRevLett.97.187401
- **Peaks:** 1580 cm⁻¹ (G band), 2700 cm⁻¹ (2D band)
- **Quality Indicators:** I(2D)/I(G) > 2, FWHM(2D) = 20-30 cm⁻¹
- **Description:** Single-layer graphene with characteristic G and 2D bands

#### **2. Graphite (Bulk)** 🔬
- **Source:** Tuinstra & Koenig, J. Chem. Phys. 53, 1126 (1970)
- **Peaks:** 1580 cm⁻¹ (G band), 2720 cm⁻¹ (2D band, broad)
- **Quality Indicators:** I(2D)/I(G) < 0.5, FWHM(2D) = 50-80 cm⁻¹
- **Description:** Bulk graphite with multiple layers

#### **3. Graphene Oxide (GO)** 🔬
- **Source:** Kudin et al., Nano Lett. 8, 36 (2008)
- **DOI:** 10.1021/nl071822y
- **Peaks:** 1350 cm⁻¹ (D band), 1590 cm⁻¹ (G band, shifted)
- **Quality Indicators:** I(D)/I(G) = 0.8-1.3 (high defect density)
- **Description:** Oxidized graphene with oxygen functional groups

#### **4. Silicon (Crystalline)** 🔬
- **Source:** NIST SRM 2241, RRUFF R040031
- **Reference:** Temple & Hathaway, Phys. Rev. B (1973)
- **DOI:** 10.1063/1.1713333
- **Peak:** 520.7 cm⁻¹ (F2g mode)
- **FWHM:** 3.5 cm⁻¹ (high crystallinity)
- **Calibration Standard:** Primary Raman calibration (NIST SRM 2241)
- **Description:** Crystalline silicon wafer, calibration standard

#### **5. Diamond** 🔬
- **Source:** RRUFF R040007, Solin & Ramdas (1970)
- **DOI:** 10.1103/PhysRevB.1.1687
- **Peak:** 1332.5 cm⁻¹ (F2g mode)
- **FWHM:** 1.8 cm⁻¹ (ultra-narrow, high quality)
- **Description:** Natural or synthetic diamond with single sharp peak

#### **6. TiO₂ (Anatase)** 🔬
- **Source:** RRUFF R060277, Ohsaka et al. (1978)
- **DOI:** 10.1016/0022-4596(78)90114-0
- **Peaks:** 144, 197, 399, 513, 519, 639 cm⁻¹ (6 modes)
- **Diagnostic Peak:** 144 cm⁻¹ (Eg(1), strongest)
- **Description:** Anatase phase titanium dioxide

#### **7. TiO₂ (Rutile)** 🔬
- **Source:** RRUFF R040031, Porto et al. (1967)
- **DOI:** 10.1103/PhysRev.154.522
- **Peaks:** 143, 447, 612 cm⁻¹ (3 modes)
- **Diagnostic Peak:** 447 cm⁻¹ (Eg, strongest)
- **Description:** Rutile phase titanium dioxide

#### **8. MoS₂ (Molybdenum Disulfide)** 🔬
- **Source:** Lee et al., ACS Nano 4, 2695 (2010)
- **DOI:** 10.1021/nn1003937
- **Peaks:** 383 cm⁻¹ (E2g), 408 cm⁻¹ (A1g)
- **Quality Indicator:** Peak separation ~25 cm⁻¹ for monolayer
- **Description:** 2D material, layer-dependent peak separation

#### **9. Polystyrene** 🔬
- **Source:** ASTM E1840, ISO 16129
- **Peaks:** 621, 1001.4, 1031.8, 1583.1, 1602.3 cm⁻¹
- **Calibration Standard:** 1001.4 cm⁻¹ (ASTM E1840)
- **Accuracy:** ±0.5 cm⁻¹
- **Description:** Primary Raman calibration standard

#### **10. Quartz (α-SiO₂)** 🔬
- **Source:** RRUFF R040031
- **Peaks:** 128, 206, 465, 1085 cm⁻¹
- **Diagnostic Peak:** 465 cm⁻¹ (strongest)
- **Description:** Alpha-quartz crystalline silica

#### **11. Calcite (CaCO₃)** 🔬
- **Source:** RRUFF R040070
- **Peaks:** 156, 282, 712, 1086 cm⁻¹
- **Diagnostic Peak:** 1086 cm⁻¹ (CO3²⁻ symmetric stretch)
- **Description:** Calcium carbonate (calcite polymorph)

```json
  }

```

**Interpretation:** High-quality monolayer graphene confirmed by:
- I(2D)/I(G) = 4.0 (>2, characteristic of monolayer)
- Narrow 2D peak (FWHM = 24 cm⁻¹)
- No D band (no defects)
